The Four Rules

✦
  1. Never share it. Whoever holds the phrase holds every account made from it. Give it to another only if you mean to give them those accounts.
  2. Keep it on paper. Write it down and store it somewhere safe. Never photograph it, nor keep it in e-mail, notes or the cloud.
  3. Know that no one will ask. No genuine service, support desk or assistant needs your phrase. Whoever asks for it is after the accounts.
  4. Type it only where you trust. Into software you trust, on your own machine; better still, approve with your wallet and type it not at all.

Should the phrase ever be exposed, move what its accounts hold at once, and use it no more.
