The fameboy demo ran on a Snapchain node we rented, with a 1000 GB disk. It's moving to a much smaller server that reads from Hypersnap's public node instead, and it's off until the move is done. It comes back at this same address. Your Farcaster account is fine: it lives on the network, not on our server.

Use fameboy now

fameboy runs on your own computer too. If yours is pointed at our node, it's offline until the demo is back. To use it now, point it at Hypersnap's public node (needs Node.js 22.18 or newer, and git):

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/eucalyptus-viminalis/create-fc/main/client/install.sh | bash -s -- https://haatz.quilibrium.com

Then run fameboy ui. Sign in with your seed phrase on that page (it stays on your computer), or with an app account of your own (fameboy app-account). Use a test account. Until our server is back, some pages pause a few seconds looking for it.

What's different

  • Reads go to Hypersnap's public node, which someone else runs. They can see which accounts are looked up.
  • Phone sign-in through @fameboyclear, image uploads and invite codes are off until the demo is back.
  • Images uploaded through the demo are saved. They come back at the same links when the demo does.
